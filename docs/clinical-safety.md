# Clinical safety language

SARA-PRAGYA is a research and clinical decision-support platform. Dhātu Sāratā is not an independent diagnostic test.

## Prohibited output

- Definitive disease diagnosis from Sāratā or AI output.
- Invented symptoms, examination findings, laboratory values, or correlations.
- Causal claims based on correlation.
- Numerical risk or confidence scores without a versioned methodology.

## Required output framing

- Distinguish recorded facts, calculated values, and AI interpretations.
- Identify missing or unverified information.
- Use preliminary language until a method is validated.
- State when evidence or correlation is not established in the current dataset.
- Recommend qualified clinical evaluation when appropriate.
- Preserve the source and version of every derived result.

The user-facing disclaimer is a product control and must not be removed from assessment and report surfaces.
